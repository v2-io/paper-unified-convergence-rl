# segments/ — alternate drafts for the *eventual* revision

**Not usable in the author-response period.** NeurIPS forbids revisions to the paper and supplementary material while the discussion is open; the submitted PDF is the basis for the decision. Anything here is for the post-decision revision or resubmission.

Drafts live here rather than in `src/` so the as-submitted tree stays intact and reviewable against `../submitted-neurips-2026.pdf` (frozen) and tag `submitted/neurips-2026`. When a draft is adopted, move it into `src/` at that time, not before.

Naming: `<issue-id>-<segment-slug>.md`, e.g. `I-11-intro-with-glosses.md`, so a draft is traceable to the issue that motivated it.
