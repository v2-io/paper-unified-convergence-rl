## Related Work ^sec-related-work

The four tracks named in [[#^sec-introduction]] organize the literature.

**Variation-budget dynamic regret.** \cite{cheung-2020-reinforcement, wei-luo-2021-blackbox, mao-2021-nearoptimal, gajane-2019-variational, cheung-simchi-levi-zhu-2022-blessing} establish dynamic regret bounds under a *variation budget* $V_T$ on cumulative reward and transition variation. \cite{mao-2021-nearoptimal} achieves the near-optimal continuous-variation rate $\tilde O(SA \, V_T^{1/3} H \, T^{2/3})$ via RestartQ-UCB; \cite{cheung-2020-reinforcement} treats the piecewise-stationary case with restart-on-change. Our cumulative rate $\tilde O(N_h \sqrt{(B_T+1) T})$ sits in the piecewise-stationary family — same block-shape as Cheung-style restart-on-change — recovered as a corollary of composition with the per-round identity. The novelty is the *threshold form* of the strategic-tempo inequality (environment regimes where no $\lambda \in (0,1)$ stabilizes the sector-Lyapunov mismatch), which dynamic-regret-optimization analyses do not surface.

**Two-term regret decompositions along the exploration-vs-adaptation axis.** \cite{li-zhao-zhou-2024-dynamic-regret, fei-2020-dynamic, stradi-2024-learning} decompose the dynamic regret into a *confidence-set construction* term and an *adaptation-under-non-stationarity* term. \cite{yang-2024-feasibility} treats a structurally adjacent question — constraint-region feasibility — using a two-term decomposition along that axis. Same shape, different axis: ours is goal-feasibility-vs-policy-quality (the satisfaction gap distinguishes "the goal is unattainable from $M_t$" from "the current policy is suboptimal"); theirs is uncertainty-source. Neither subsumes the other: a Long-Fei Li-style exploration term can be present in any of our 2$\times$2 cells, and an adaptation term likewise. The two axes are orthogonal.

**Tempo and forgetting.** \cite{lee-2023-prost-tempo} (ProST) defines an agent tempo as the schedule of policy update times $\{t_1, \ldots, t_K\}$ and computes the schedule that minimizes a dynamic regret upper bound. \cite{lee-2024-pausing} (Pausing Policy Learning) refines this with non-zero hold durations. \cite{touati-2020-efficient, russac-2019-weighted, garivier-2008-upperconfidence} establish forgetting / sliding-window analyses for non-stationary linear MDPs and bandits. We *lift* the ProST move along two axes simultaneously: single-factor → multi-factor (per-element $\nu \cdot \iota \cdot (1-\lambda)$ rather than scalar update frequency); and hyperparameter-optimization → structural-survival inequality (the threshold below which no schedule persists, irrespective of which schedule is chosen). ProST recovers as the Beta-Bernoulli special case $|E| = 1$, $\nu = \iota = 1$.

**Causal and interventional access.** \cite{zhang-2016-mdps, zhang-2022-online-rl, lu-2021-causal, lu-2022-efficient, wang-2021-provably, zhang-2020-designing, schulte-poupart-2025-causal-rl} use causal-graph structure to sharpen sample complexity in RL. Their setting is *stationary*. We compose the closed-loop interventional-access argument with non-stationarity. The connection between Pearl's causal hierarchy \cite{pearl-2009-causality} and the data-generating process of an agent in a feedback loop \cite{bareinboim-correa-ibeling-icard-2022-pearl-hierarchy} is the technical bridge.

**Information-theoretic regret (cross-cutting).** \cite{russo-2014-informationtheoretic, russo-vanroy-2014-ids, lu-2019-informationtheoretic, min-russo-2023-nonstat-bandit, lattimore-2021-mirror, canonne-2022-short, kakade-2020-information-online} traverse the above tracks at a different layer, using entropy / mutual information / information ratio / Pinsker / Hellinger as the divergence backbone. The Russo–Van Roy information ratio uses Pinsker as its Cauchy–Schwarz follow-up; the IDS line and its descendants use mutual-information bounds. The Bretagnolle–Huber inequality \cite{bretagnolle-huber-1978-densities} — well-known in lower-bound proofs via Le Cam's method \cite{tsybakov-2009-nonparametric, lattimore-2020-bandit} — does not appear *as an upper-bound regret coordinate* in this corpus, and its exact point-mass corner (our Lemma [[#^lem-pointmass-identity]]) is *a fortiori* absent. We document the retrieval scope and methodology in [[#^sec-prior-art]].

**Adjacent (satisficing / feasibility).** \cite{hajiabolhassan-2025-online, zhang-2026-peril} use *satisficing* to mean "any policy above acceptance level $\beta$ is acceptable." Our $\delta_{\mathrm{sat}}$ is structurally distinct: $V_O^{\min}$ is a property of the objective (set by the application domain), and $\delta_{\mathrm{sat}} > 0$ signals goal-relative *infeasibility* (no policy in $\Pi$ meets the threshold), not policy-relative satisficing.

**Contemporaneous (post-March 2026).** \cite{gerogiannis-2026-darling, zhang-2026-peril}; cited and distinguished without empirical-comparison demand per the contemporaneous-work cutoff convention.
