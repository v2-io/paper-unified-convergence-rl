## Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

The four-component composition delivers three properties no published strand has individually: *handles non-stationarity* (strategic-tempo persistence), *explicit metric structure on policy space* (point-mass identity), *on-policy learnable* (closed-loop interventional access). The cumulative regret rate is the Best-of-Both-Worlds form $\tilde O(\min\{V_{\max} N_h \sqrt{(B_T+1) T},\, V_{\max} N_h (V_T+1)^{1/3} T^{2/3}\})$ — automatically adapting between piecewise-stationary $B_T$ and continuous variation $V_T$ via Wei-Luo MASTER wrapping ([[#^sec-proof-composition]] (A5')-BoBW Remark), with per-round identity supplying a coordinate that's exact at the deterministic-$\pi^*$ corner.

**On structural unifiability of the variation regimes.** The $B_T$ and $V_T$ regimes share the per-round coordinate; they differ in aggregation strategy (block-Cauchy–Schwarz vs. adaptive-window MASTER). The $V_T$ exponent matches \cite{mao-2021-nearoptimal}'s near-optimal rate, and \cite{besbes-gur-zeevi-2014-stochastic}'s lower bound applies at the deterministic-$\pi^*$ corner — so the per-round identity sharpens constants and per-round form, not the $V_T$ exponent. Two refinements remain open: an *agent-experienced* variation budget $V_T^{\mathrm{eff},\Sigma}$ counting only the times $\|\boldsymbol\delta_\Sigma\|$ exits its ultimate-bounded region, and an *identity-coordinate* variation $V_T^{(K)} := \sum_t |K_t - K_{t-1}|$ — both bounded above by environment-side $V_T$ but typically much smaller in absorbing regimes where the strategic substate damps drift. The result would explain why agents in slow-drift environments outperform $B_T$-pessimistic predictions.

**On coupled-goal architectures.** (C2)'s sequential-ignorability presupposes architectural separation between model state $M_t$ and goal state. Goal-conditioned LLM policies violate this: the goal influences both action selection and environment response via shared latent context (user intent, dialogue history, task framing), so $H_t$ does not block the goal-mediated path from $a_t$ to $o_{t+1}$ — (C2) fails. \cite{bruineberg-dolega-dewhurst-baltieri-2022-bbs}'s conditional-independence relaxation is a starting point.

**Open scope questions.** *Optimal $N_h$, $S$, $A$ dependence:* the lifted UCRL2/UCBVI rate $\tilde O(N_h^2\sqrt{SA(B_T+1)T})$ could shave one $\sqrt{N_h}$ factor via Bernstein-type concentration \cite{azar-2017-minimax}. *Joint uniqueness:* metric layer is uniquely reverse-KL up to scaling under chain-rule additivity ([[#^thm-chain-rule-uniqueness]]); we do not claim joint uniqueness across all three properties. *Strategic-tempo beyond Model (Σ):* the bottleneck threshold is sharp inside the diagonal sector model; non-diagonal correction is open in the adaptive-control–RL bridge.

**Practitioner takeaway.** Use the aggregate necessary-condition $\mathcal T_\Sigma^{\mathrm{agg,ss}} > |E| \cdot \rho_\Sigma/R_\Sigma$ as a fail-fast pre-check; estimate $\rho_\Sigma/R_\Sigma$ from observed variation \cite{cheung-2020-reinforcement, wei-luo-2021-blackbox, gerogiannis-2026-darling}; route corrective action via the 2$\times$2 diagnostic; apply the per-round identity wherever deterministic-$\pi^*$ scope holds. Regime C (observation-only) flags itself as out-of-scope.
